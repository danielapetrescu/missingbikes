# missingbikes.dk

## Why I'm building this

Riding my bike through the city, I saw so many bikes just thrown 
away. Nice bikes. Then I saw in the local group so many posts 
showing found bikes. Some found their owners. Most didn't.

And it hurt me, because I knew that somewhere, someone was missing 
it. Maybe it was someone for whom that bike meant more — a way to 
get to work, a way to make a living, or just how they got around 
every day.

When the bikes on the local group didn't find their owners, I 
realized why — the bikes clearly came from other neighbourhoods. 
People just weren't looking in the right place.

I found one myself, thrown away in a lake. I took it, washed it, and 
posted about it on every group. I even called the police. But 
unfortunately, the bike never made it back to its owner, even though 
I did everything I could think of.

So I thought — if there was a site, one that was known, that 
everyone knew about, then no matter where you lived, in any 
neighbourhood, you'd be able to find your bike if someone posted it 
there.

I really want to help people get their bikes back.

## What it does

- Found a bike? Post it with a photo and its location.
- Lost your bike? Check the list to see if someone found it.

## Status

🚧 In progress — currently building Sprint 1 (add a bike + view the 
list of found bikes). Following an Agile SDLC process, with planning, 
analysis, and design docs for each sprint in [`docs/`](./docs).

## Tech stack

- Java, Spring Boot
- PostgreSQL
- Google Maps API (location capture)
- Self-hosted on AWS EC2
